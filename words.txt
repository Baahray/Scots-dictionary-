#   - several Scots words go in the second part, separated by commas
#   - "how to say it" is spelled out the way an English reader would sound it,
#     one for each Scots word, in the same order, separated by commas
#   - the note is optional (e.g. Glasgow, East, old)
#   - lines starting with # are ignored
#   - the site sorts everything A–Z itself, so new lines can go anywhere
#
# Example:   cold | cauld | cawld
