# Topics: groups of Scots words that share a meaning (e.g. all the ways to say "hit").
#
# ONE TOPIC PER LINE:   id | title | English words that bring it up in search | short intro
#
# To put a word in a topic, add  "topic: id"  to the note of its line in words.txt, e.g.
#   smack, slap | skelp | skelp | verb & noun – topic: hitting | Ah skelped him wi ma shoe. (I hit him with my shoe.)
